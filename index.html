An artist-led research and design initiative exploring how an intentionally designed home might better support life with multiple sclerosis.

Meet PSMS-01 ↓

01 / The vision

What if the environment were designed around the nervous system?

Paradigm Shift MS begins with lived experience: heat, light, sound, movement, fatigue, access, and the spaces between them are not abstract design concerns. They shape daily life.

The project brings art, architecture, neuroaesthetics, environmental design, technology, and published research into one question: can a home be composed to reduce friction and make more room for living?

02 / The first prototype

PSMS is the initiative.
PSMS-01 is where it becomes real.

PSMS-01 is the proposed first full-scale home, creative work, and long-term learning environment. Its design is being developed as an integrated system—not a collection of wellness products.

Architecture, access, environmental control, art, sound, light, nature, and regenerative infrastructure will be evaluated together with qualified professional collaborators as the project advances.

  • 01EnvironmentThermal, humidity, air, light, and acoustic conditions
  • 02AccessMovement, rest, orientation, safety, and reduced friction
  • 03NeuroartsArt, sound, form, and sensory experience as design inputs
  • 04RegenerationResilient, lower-impact energy, water, and living systems
  • 05LearningDocumented hypotheses, design decisions, and future observation

03 / Evidence and integrity

No cure claims.
No borrowed certainty.

PSMS distinguishes published evidence, lived experience, working hypotheses, design responses, and future results. Cited research can inform a requirement without proving the complete environment will produce a particular outcome.

The project will say what is known, what is proposed, what remains unresolved, and where each meaningful contribution came from.

05 / How this is being made

Human vision. Lived experience. Existing knowledge. AI-assisted development.

Paradigm Shift MS originates with CASEY MARIE ROY / @SPACECASE135 — artist and MS advocate. Casey provides the concept, lived-experience basis, independent research, creative direction, requirements, decisions, corrections, and final judgment.

AI assists with synthesis, drafting, organization, coding, visualization, and development. Published researchers and future architects, engineers, builders, clinicians, and other collaborators retain clear credit for what they contribute.

This provenance is not a footnote. It is part of the project's integrity.
